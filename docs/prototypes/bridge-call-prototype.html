1 · Ask before touching anything

Jarvis's first move is 3 narrowing questions — zero sweeps, zero engineers engaged. Last night's real reply swept the whole APIC first and asked last.

2 · Call-length messages

Every Jarvis line fits a bridge call: 1–2 sentences. Evidence is folded away until you expand it — no walls of text.

3 · Who's on the call

The bridge card shows who Jarvis pulled in and who was stood down — like a real call roster. Only the ACI engineer works this one.

4 · Black-screen CLI

Command evidence renders as an SSH session — prompt, real command, colored output — not prose paragraphs.

5 · The narrowing loop

Jarvis tasks an engineer, reads the answer, states what it means in one line, then asks the next question — until cause is found.

6 · Fix goes through change

The fix is drafted as a change record and HELD for approval — Jarvis never touches devices on its own.

7 · Pick the look

V1: everything in the chat. V2: chat left + a persistent SSH pane right. V3: compact war-room timeline. Which one (or a mix)?
